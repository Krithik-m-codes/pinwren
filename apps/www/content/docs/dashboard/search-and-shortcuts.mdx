## The command palette

Press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd>) on any screen, or click the search field in the
sidebar. It goes to screens, projects and [saved views](/docs/dashboard/saved-views), and runs a few actions:
**Customise dashboard**, **Keyboard shortcuts** and **Copy link to this page**.

### Search feedback

After two characters the palette searches your feedback in all statuses, using the same search as the inbox
(`#12`, words from the title or text, the reporter, part of the page URL). Up to six hits show with their number and
project; choose one to open it. **Search all feedback for "…"** opens the inbox with that search applied. You only
ever see what your role can see in the inbox.

### Recent items

With nothing typed, the palette lists the **Recent** items you opened, the latest eight. They are remembered in
this browser, per person; only the number, title and project name are stored, and opening one loads the item
afresh, so a deleted or moved item shows the usual not-found state.

## Shortcuts on every screen

Press `?` anywhere (or choose **Keyboard shortcuts** in the palette) for the list. Shortcuts are ignored while you
type in a field.

| Keys    | Goes to       |
| ------- | ------------- |
| `g` `i` | Inbox         |
| `g` `d` | Dashboard     |
| `g` `p` | Projects      |
| `g` `n` | Notifications |
| `g` `s` | Settings      |
| `g` `m` | My work       |

Press `g`, then the letter within a second. The inbox adds its own keys, listed under
[keyboard shortcuts](/docs/dashboard#keyboard-shortcuts).

## Start page

**Settings › Profile › Appearance › Start page** sets what opens when you visit the site or sign in: the **Inbox**
(the default), the **Dashboard** or **My work**. It is saved in your account, so it follows you to every browser (a cookie on
each device keeps the redirect instant).
