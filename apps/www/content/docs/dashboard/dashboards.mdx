Pinwren has a **Dashboard** (first entry in the sidebar, `/dashboard`) and a collapsible **Overview** strip at the
top of the inbox, the projects list, a project and notifications. They show the same widgets: numbers, charts and
lists computed from your workspace's feedback.

## What is on them

| Group       | Widgets                                                                                                                               |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Numbers** | Open items, New reports (with the change against the previous range), Resolved, Unassigned, Overdue, My work, Urgent, Time to resolve |
| **Charts**  | Reported vs resolved per day, By status, Open by type, Open by priority, Open by project                                              |
| **Lists**   | Recent activity, Projects (open and total per project), Needs attention (overdue, urgent and unowned work in one list)                |

The overview strip on the inbox starts collapsed; on a project it follows that project, elsewhere the whole
workspace. Charts are drawn without a chart library and each has a text equivalent for screen readers.

Admins start with the workspace view (unassigned work, the projects table); editors and viewers start with their
own work and what needs attention.

## Customise

Choose **Customise** (or **Customise dashboard** in the [command palette](/docs/dashboard/search-and-shortcuts)):

- **Add widget** opens a list of the widgets that are not on the dashboard yet, by group.
- **Move** a widget by dragging it, or with its **Move earlier** and **Move later** buttons (the buttons also work
  with a keyboard).
- **Resize** it: width of a quarter, third, half, two thirds or the full row (a 12-column grid), and a short, medium
  or tall height.
- **Remove** a widget you do not need.
- **Reset to default** brings back the layout for your role. **Done** leaves edit mode.

The **Date range** (last 7, 14, 30 or 90 days, or a custom span from the calendar) sets the period for the tiles
and charts. The preset is saved with the layout; a custom span lives in the link.

<Callout title="Layouts follow your account">
  Your dashboards are stored on the server, per person and per screen, so they follow you to every browser and
  device. The first time you open a dashboard after upgrading, the layout your browser kept is imported once
  as **My dashboard**. Until you save something you see the built-in layout for your role, so later
  improvements to the defaults still reach you. Viewers can customise too: it only changes their own
  dashboards.
</Callout>

## Several dashboards

The dashboard name next to **Customise** opens the picker. Keep as many dashboards as you like per screen (up to
25), for example **Triage** and **Exec weekly**:

- **New dashboard…** starts from the layout you are looking at and opens it. Each dashboard has its own link
  (`/dashboard?dash=…`).
- **Rename**, **Duplicate** and **Delete** act on the open dashboard. Names are unique for you on a screen.
- **Open by default** makes it the one that opens on that screen; choose it again to stop.
- The overview strips on the inbox, projects, a project and notifications have the same picker.

### Sharing with the workspace

An Admin can open **Share with workspace…** on any of their dashboards. Everyone in the workspace then sees it under
**Shared with the workspace**, read-only. They choose **Duplicate to my dashboards** (or **Customise**, which does
the same) to get a copy they can change. Admins can also edit or remove a shared dashboard, and unsharing hides it
again. People who already copied it keep their copies.

In the same dialog an Admin can make a shared dashboard the starting point for **Admins**, **Editors** or **Viewers**.
Anyone in those roles who has not made a dashboard of their own then starts there instead of on the built-in
layout; a role has one such dashboard per screen.

## Filter bar

The home dashboard has a filter bar above the widgets: **Project**, **Label** (of the chosen project),
**Assignee** (anyone, you, unassigned or a teammate) and the **Date range**. Every number, chart and list follows
it. The filters are kept in the address bar, so you can copy the link (**Copy link**) and send a filtered view to a
colleague; **Clear filters** removes them. Review rounds follow only the project filter.

## Also on your account

The start page (Settings › Profile) and the dismissed update and support prompts follow your account too, and you
can pin projects in the sidebar (hover a project, then the pin). If your session expires while a screen is open,
Pinwren says so and takes you to sign-in, then back to the same page and filters.

## The stats endpoint

Everything on a screen comes from one request, so moving or resizing a widget never reloads data.

```http
GET /api/stats?days=14&projectId=<uuid>&labelId=<uuid>&assigneeId=me&from=2026-03-01&to=2026-03-31
```

| Parameter    | Description                                                                                                         |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `days`       | The range: `7`, `14` (default), `30` or `90`.                                                                       |
| `from`, `to` | An explicit span of UTC dates (`YYYY-MM-DD`, inclusive, at most 366 days; `to` defaults to today). Replaces `days`. |
| `projectId`  | Optional. Limit the numbers to one project; a project of another workspace matches nothing.                         |
| `labelId`    | Optional. Only items with this label; a label of another workspace matches nothing.                                 |
| `assigneeId` | Optional. `me`, `none` (unassigned) or a member id.                                                                 |

The response holds `totals` (`active`, `created`, `previousCreated`, `resolved`, `unassigned`, `overdue`, `mine`,
`urgent`, `avgResolutionHours`), `byStatus`, `byKind`, `byPriority`, `byProject` (only without `projectId`), a
zero-filled daily `series` of `created` and `resolved` (oldest first, UTC days, ending on `to` when it is set) and the latest `recent` activity.
Trashed items are not counted. The call needs a dashboard session and reads only your own workspace; the token API
has the same numbers and filters at `GET /api/v1/stats` (SDK `stats.get`, MCP `get_stats`). The dashboard refreshes the numbers when the [live stream](/docs/dashboard#live-updates)
reports a change.

## Not built yet

A per-card editor and funnel and gauge charts are not available. The inbox filters cover ad-hoc slicing.
