Page review answers "what's wrong with _this_ page?" rather than "what's in my queue?". Open a project and choose
**Pages** (`/projects/<id>/pages`).

## All pages

The overview lists every page of the project that has feedback, newest activity first, each with a thumbnail of its
latest screenshot and its open and closed counts. The view reads up to 500 of the project's most recent items:
enough for a review, not an archive. Use the [inbox](/docs/dashboard) search for anything older.

### How pages are grouped

Pins left on `/pricing`, `/pricing/` and `/pricing?utm_source=newsletter` belong to one page. Before grouping, a
page address loses:

- the hash, and a trailing slash (the site root keeps its `/`);
- campaign and tracking parameters: `utm_*`, `gclid`, `fbclid`, `msclkid`, `mc_cid`, `mc_eid`, `ref`;
- Pinwren's own link parameters (`pinwren`, `pwt`, `pinwren_review`);
- the parameters you list in **URL grouping**.

The remaining query parameters are sorted, so `?b=2&a=1` and `?a=1&b=2` are the same page.

**URL grouping** (top right of the overview, Admins only) lists parameters that don't identify a page on your
site, such as a session id (`sid`) or an A/B flag. End a name with `*` to match a prefix (`hsa_*`); up to 30.
**Save and re-group** applies the rule to new feedback and merges the pages already stored. Removing a parameter
later doesn't split pages that were merged.

## One page

Pick a page to see:

- **The capture** — a screenshot of the page with every pin and region drawn where it was left. Bug reports and,
  with **Screenshot with every comment** on, comments carry the viewport their screenshot shows (width, height,
  scroll position), so every thread made at the same screen width lines up with it. Older items are placed using
  the geometry stored in their [anchor](/docs/widget/anchoring).
- **Annotations** — when the reporter drew on the screenshot (boxes, arrows, highlights, numbered steps, text), the
  marks are drawn over the capture. The **Annotations** toggle hides them to show what was underneath. Areas the
  reporter blurred stay blurred: they are part of the image.
- **Other captures** — when several items carry a screenshot, choose which one to use as the base. By default it is
  the capture on which the most threads can be placed (newest wins a tie). A cropped screenshot or a capture of one
  element shows no whole viewport, so it can't be used as a base.
- **Threads on this page** — every item with its kind, status and number. Items whose anchor doesn't fit the chosen
  capture are listed as "Not on this capture" instead of being drawn in the wrong place; bug reports and comments
  on the whole page are listed as "Whole page".
- **Show: Open / All** — only open threads, or resolved ones too.
- **Open page** — the live page.

Click a thread to open it in the [detail panel](/docs/dashboard#list-and-detail).

<Callout title="No capture yet?">
  Turn on **Screenshot with every comment** on the project's **Capture** tab: every new comment then sends a
  masked screenshot of what the commenter saw, and the page gets a capture with its first comment. Bug reports
  always carry one unless the reporter removed it.
</Callout>

## On the live page

**Open on page** (in the detail panel here and in the inbox) opens the page with a signed link
(`?pinwren=<id>&pwt=<token>`, valid for 7 days). The widget scrolls to the pin and opens its thread, even when the
project keeps pins private. Anyone with the link can read that one item and its public replies, so share it like
the item itself.

### Re-pin

When the page changed and a pin points at the wrong spot (the widget marks it **Moved?**), Admins and Editors can
use **Re-pin on page**. It opens the page with a re-pin link, valid for 24 hours: a banner says
"Click where #12 belongs", and the next click (or drag, for an area) moves the pin. **Cancel** or <kbd>Esc</kbd>
leaves it where it was. The old position stays in the item's history. Tools and scripts can do the same with
`PATCH /api/feedback/:id/anchor`.
