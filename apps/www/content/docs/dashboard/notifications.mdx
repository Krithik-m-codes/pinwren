title: Notifications
description: In-app and email notifications for your team, per-project preferences, and emails to the people who reported.
---

Pinwren tells the right people when something happens — in the dashboard's bell, by email, or both. Notifications
are sent by background jobs, so a slow or broken mail server never delays a report.

## What your team is told

| Notification       | Who gets it                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **New feedback**   | Members who follow new feedback on the project                                                                                                                                                 |
| **Assigned**       | The member something was just assigned to                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Reply**          | People involved in the item — its assignees and team members who already commented. A visitor's reply on an item nobody is involved in yet goes to everyone who follows replies on the project |
| **Status changed** | The item's assignees                                                                                                                                                                           |

Nobody is notified about their own action. The bell in the sidebar shows unread notifications with the project,
number and title, links to the item, and has **Mark all read**. Read notifications are cleared after 90 days.

## Preferences

Each member chooses, per project, in **Settings › Notifications**:

| Column           | Meaning                             |
| ---------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| **New feedback** | Every new comment or bug report     |
| **Assigned**     | Something is assigned to you        |
| **Replies**      | Replies on items you're involved in |
| **Status**       | Status changes on your items        |
| **Email**        | Also send these by email            |

Everything is on by default. In-app notifications are always written for what you follow; **Email** decides whether
they are also mailed. Emails need SMTP — see [email](/docs/integrations/email). Without it, emails are skipped and
the bell still works.

## Emails to reporters

When a reporter leaves an email address in the widget and the project has **Email the reporter** on (project
settings › **Privacy & data**, `notifyReporter`), they get an email when:

- the team posts a **public** reply on their feedback (internal notes never leave the dashboard), or
- the team changes its **status** — not when they resolved it themselves from the page.

Both emails use the "Reply to the reporter" and "Status update to the reporter" templates, which you can edit — see
[settings](/docs/dashboard/settings#email-templates).

<NewIn>
  Reporter emails carry a signed one-click unsubscribe link (`/api/unsubscribe?token=…`) and
  `List-Unsubscribe` headers. An address that unsubscribes goes on the workspace's suppression list, which
  every reporter email checks before it is sent.
</NewIn>

## API

Under `/api`, with a dashboard session:

| Endpoint                            | Does                                                                                                    |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `GET /api/notifications`            | `{ items, unreadCount }`; `?unread=1` for unread only, `?limit=` up to 100 (default 50)                 |
| `POST /api/notifications/read`      | `{ "ids": [...] }` or `{ "all": true }` → `{ updated }`                                                 |
| `GET /api/notification-preferences` | One row per project: `{ projectId, projectName, newFeedback, assigned, replies, statusChanges, email }` |
| `PUT /api/notification-preferences` | `{ projectId, ...any of the five booleans }`                                                            |

Notification types are `feedback.created`, `feedback.assigned`, `feedback.status_changed` and `comment.created`.
