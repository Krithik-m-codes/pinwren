title: Inbox & keyboard
description: Triage every comment and bug report in one keyboard-first inbox — list, detail, filters, bulk edits and undo.

Everything the widget collects lands in **Inbox**. It shows all projects of your workspace, or one project when you
pick it in the sidebar (`/inbox?project=<id>`). The inbox has two layouts — the list described here and the
[board](/docs/dashboard/board) — and every filter lives in the URL, so a link to a filtered inbox opens the same view
for a teammate.

## List and detail

The list shows each item's status, number, title or first line, kind (Question, Change, Bug or Other), project, page
path, a check mark for a [verified reporter](/docs/widget/identity), priority, assignee, comment count, age, and
icons for a screenshot, attachments and a session replay. Teammates who have the item open right now show as small
avatars on the row. Click a row (or press `Enter`) to open it in the detail panel beside the list; on narrow screens
and in the board it opens as a sheet. **Density** in the **Filter** menu switches between comfortable and compact
rows (remembered per device).

The detail panel holds:

- The **title**: click it (or press `r`) to rename the item; `Enter` saves, `Esc` cancels, an empty title falls back
  to the first line of the report.
- **Status, priority, assignees** (any number of teammates), **labels** (`l`), a **due date** (overdue dates show in
  red while the item is open), and a **review round** picker when the project has
  [rounds](/docs/dashboard/review-rounds).
- The reporter's name, with **Verified** when the identity was signed by your app, and avatars of teammates who have
  the item open.
- **Follow** / **Unfollow**: followers get the item's status, priority and reply notifications.
- **Copy link** (a dashboard link to the item) and **Open on page**, which opens the live page with the thread
  showing. The link is signed and works for 7 days, so it opens the thread even when pins are private on the project.
- **Duplicate of #12** under the title when the item is a duplicate, and **Duplicates: #14, #20** on the original.
- The screenshot (annotated, if the reporter drew on it) and, for bug reports that carry one, the
  [session replay](#session-replay) — as **Screenshot** and **Replay** tabs when there are both — and the attachments.
- Tabs for **Details** (page, browser, viewport, locale, reporter email, user id, consent, and **From your site**:
  the metadata your page attached with `Pinwren.setMetadata()`),
  **Anchor** (element, selector, XPath, text and position the pin is attached to), **Console** and **Network**.
- Panel actions and **Send to GitHub / GitLab** when the project has them — see
  [panel actions](/docs/widget/panel-actions) and [GitHub](/docs/integrations/github).
- The activity timeline and the comment box.
- **More actions**: copy the number or the page URL, **Copy as Markdown** (a reproduction write-up: page,
  environment, element, steps from the replay, failing requests and console errors, ready for an issue or an AI
  agent), **Rename**, **Change kind**, **Mark duplicate of…** (search by number or words; sets the status to
  Duplicate), and, for Admins, **Move to trash**.

Opening an item marks its notifications in the bell as read.

## Trash

Only Admins delete. A deleted item moves to the trash and the toast offers **Undo**. Admins open the trash with the
**Trash** button in the inbox header (`/inbox?trash=1`): items show when they were deleted, and **Restore** or
**Delete forever** work on one item (from its detail) or on a selection. Items are purged with their files 30 days
after they were deleted. Restoring puts the item back in everyone's inbox and is recorded in its timeline.

## Statuses, priorities, kinds

| Field    | Values                                                                             |
| -------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Status   | Open, In progress, In review, Resolved, Won't fix, Duplicate                       |
| Priority | No priority, Low, Medium, High, Urgent                                             |
| Kind     | Question, Change, Bug or Other (set by the reporter; change it under More actions) |

Open, In progress and In review are the **active** statuses; Resolved, Won't fix and Duplicate are **closed**. Closed
items leave the page in the widget (visitors can still show resolved pins) and close a linked GitHub, GitLab, Linear or Jira issue.

## Filters and search

- **Active / Resolved / All** tabs filter by status.
- **Filter** menu: **Kind**, **Priority**, **Assignee** (anyone, me, or a member), **Label**, **Review round** and
  **Review link** (when one project is chosen; review links for admins) and **Created** (last 24 hours, 7 days or 30 days). Each active filter shows as a chip under the
  toolbar; remove one with its ×, or all with **Clear**.
- **Search** (`/`) matches the beginning of words in the title and text, an item number (`#12`), the reporter's
  name or email, and part of the page URL. Press `Enter` to apply. When nothing in the current tab matches,
  **Search all statuses** looks in closed items too; when filters hide everything, **Clear filters** brings it back.
- **Sort**: newest or oldest first, recently updated, highest or lowest priority.
- **My work** in the sidebar (or `g` then `m`) shows the items assigned to you in every project.
- The list loads more items as you scroll and shows how many match (**Showing 50 of 312**).
- **Project** — choose a project in the sidebar.

The URL parameters are `project`, `status`, `kind`, `priority`, `assignee`, `round`, `page` (part of the page URL),
`since` (ISO date), `label`, `reporter`, `review` (a [review link](/docs/dashboard/settings#review-links) id), `sort` (`created`, `updated`, `priority`), `order` (`asc`, `desc`), `q`,
`trash=1` and `layout=board`; `assignee=me` is whoever opens the link. **Save view** keeps the current filters and layout as a
[saved view](/docs/dashboard/saved-views).

## Bulk edits and undo

Tick rows with their checkbox or `x` (Shift-click or `Shift` + `x` selects a range). A bar appears at the bottom to
set the status or priority, assign or unassign, add or remove labels (items of one project), add the selection to an
active review round or take it out (when one project is chosen), **Export** it, or (Admins) delete it. Every change
shows a toast with **Undo**, and `u` undoes the last change from anywhere in the inbox: each item goes back to its
own previous value.

## Export

**Export** in the toolbar downloads every item matching the current filters and sort, all pages, as **CSV** (opens in
Excel and Google Sheets), **JSON** or **PDF** (the first 1,000). The bulk bar's **Export** downloads only the selected
items. Each row has the number, project, kind, status, priority, title, text, reporter, page, labels, assignees,
created, updated and due dates, and a link to the item. The API is `GET /api/feedback/export?format=csv|json|pdf`
with the list filters, or `ids=<id>,<id>`.

## Labels

Labels belong to a project. Manage them in **Project settings › Labels** (name, colour, how many items carry each),
or create one straight from the label picker on an item by typing a new name. Admins and Editors manage labels;
Viewers see them. Deleting a label removes it from its items.

## Comments: internal or public

The comment box has two modes:

| Mode             | Who sees it                                                                                                                            |
| ---------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Internal**     | Only your team, in the dashboard. Highlighted in amber. The default.                                                                   |
| **Public reply** | The reporter: in the pin's thread on the page when pins are public, and by email when reporter emails are on and they left an address. |

Under **Public reply** a line says where the reply will go (on the page, by email, or **Not delivered** with a link
to the project setting). Type `@` to mention a teammate and pick them from the list (arrows and `Enter`): they get a
notification and an email. `Ctrl` + `Enter` (`⌘` + `Enter` on a Mac) sends. Visitors' replies from the page show up here as public comments. You can edit your own comments; authors and
workspace admins can delete them, and the timeline keeps a note that a comment was deleted.

## Activity timeline

Every item records who did what: created (from the widget), status, priority, title, kind, label, due date,
duplicate and assignee changes (with the before and after values), moves to and from the trash, comments, deleted comments, issue links and panel-action runs. Changes made through the
[API](/docs/api) show the token's owner with "via API"; changes from GitHub, GitLab, Linear or Jira show the tracker.

## Keyboard shortcuts

Press `?` in the inbox (or the keyboard button in its header) for this list. Shortcuts are ignored while you type in
a field, and viewers' shortcuts don't change anything (`x`, `e`, `s`, `p`, `a`, `l`, `r`, `c` and `u` are off).

| Key             | Action                                                 |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| `⌘K` / `Ctrl K` | Command palette: go to a screen, project or saved view |
| `j` / `k`       | Next / previous item (also `↓` / `↑`)                  |
| `Enter`         | Open the focused item                                  |
| `Esc`           | Close the item, then clear the selection               |
| `x`             | Select the open item (`Shift` for a range)             |
| `e`             | Resolve / reopen                                       |
| `s`             | Status menu                                            |
| `p`             | Priority menu                                          |
| `a`             | Assign menu                                            |
| `l`             | Labels menu                                            |
| `r`             | Rename the item                                        |
| `g` `m`         | My work (items assigned to you)                        |
| `c`             | Comment                                                |
| `u`             | Undo the last change                                   |
| `/`             | Search                                                 |
| `m`             | Move a board card to another column                    |
| `?`             | Show the shortcuts                                     |

## Live updates

The inbox is live. The dashboard keeps one Server-Sent Events connection to `GET /api/stream` open and applies
changes as they happen: new items, status, priority and assignee changes, deletions and restores, and new, edited
and deleted comments; an item that a change moves out of the current filter leaves the list, from teammates,
the widget, the API and issue trackers alike. New items slide in at the top while the list is scrolled to the top;
when you've scrolled down, an **N new** pill appears instead — click it to jump up and show them. If the connection
drops, the dashboard reconnects with backoff and refetches the list so nothing is missed.

**Presence**: while you have an item open, your avatar shows on that row and in the item's header for everyone else
looking at the inbox, so two people don't triage the same report. The dashboard sends a heartbeat every 20 seconds
(`POST /api/presence`); you disappear 45 seconds after the last one.

The stream sends `feedback.created`, `feedback.updated`, `feedback.deleted`, `comment.created`, `comment.updated`,
`comment.deleted`, `presence` and a `ping`
every 25 seconds (optional `projectIds=` query). It closes after 15 minutes so the session is checked again, and the
browser reconnects with `Last-Event-ID`. Behind a reverse proxy, turn response buffering off for `/api/stream` — see
[reverse proxy](/docs/self-hosting/reverse-proxy).
