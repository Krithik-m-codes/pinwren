---
title: Roles and permissions
description: Define custom roles from a catalogue of permissions, assign them per person, and how they behave on downgrade. An Enterprise licence feature.
---

Admin, Editor and Viewer cover most teams. When someone needs a different mix, such as "can triage and comment but
cannot delete", "can read the audit log" or "can add users but only Viewers", define a **custom role**. Custom roles
are an Enterprise feature: you need an [Enterprise licence](/docs/licensing) that includes `custom_roles` when you
self-host. They are not part of any Pinwren Cloud plan. The built-in roles are always there and work the same with or
without a licence.

## Create a role

Open **Settings → Roles** (workspace Admins only). Choose **New role**, name it, and tick the permissions it grants.
Names are unique per workspace, ignoring case. Reading feedback is always included.

| Group              | Permissions                                                                                              |
| ------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Feedback           | view, create manually, edit/assign/bulk-update, comment, delete (and rounds), restore and view the trash |
| Workspace          | manage projects, labels, integrations and webhooks, workspace settings, branding and email templates     |
| Access and billing | manage users, read the audit log, manage billing                                                         |

Only permissions the server actually enforces are listed. Two things can never be granted to a custom role:
managing roles (Admins only) and instance administration (the `/api/admin` area and the licence). Your own
workspace **owner** and Admins are never affected by a custom role.

## Assign a role

On **Settings → Users**, the role picker of each person (and the one in **Add user**) lists the built-in roles and
your custom ones. Invitations by email still offer the three built-in roles; assign a custom role after the person
joins. Changing someone back to Admin, Editor or Viewer removes their custom role.

**Nobody can grant what they do not hold.** A person with the _manage users_ permission can assign a role only if
they hold every permission in it, and can change or remove someone only if they hold everything that person can do.
So a users manager cannot create an Admin or demote one. The owner can never be demoted or removed, you cannot change
your own role, and a workspace always keeps at least one Admin.

## How it is enforced

Every check goes through one resolver on the server. A member with a custom role gets exactly the listed permissions,
and a route that does not name a permission is closed to them. Their API tokens, the MCP server and Slack actions act
as the same person and are limited the same way. The dashboard hides what a role cannot do (it reads the resolved
list from `GET /api/me`), but the server is the authority.

Behind the scenes a custom-role member keeps a built-in base role: **Viewer** if the role only reads (feedback and the
audit log), **Editor** otherwise. It is updated whenever the role changes.

## Editing, deleting and downgrades

- Editing a role changes its members' access at once.
- A role that people still hold cannot be deleted; give them another role first (`409 ROLE_IN_USE`).
- If the licence lapses or the feature is removed, custom roles stay stored but stop applying: their members fall
  back to the permissions of their Editor or Viewer base role, nobody is locked out, and Admins are untouched. You
  cannot create, edit or assign roles until the feature returns (an expired licence in its read-only period keeps
  roles working but frozen). Moving a person to a built-in role always works.

## Audit trail

With the [audit log](/docs/dashboard/audit-log) active, creating, changing and deleting a role and assigning one to a
person are recorded, including which permissions were added or removed.

## API

`GET /api/roles` (admins and users managers), `POST /api/roles`, `PATCH /api/roles/:id` and `DELETE /api/roles/:id`
(admins). Assign with `PATCH /api/members/:userId` and `{ "customRoleId": "…" }` (or `{ "role": "editor" }` to go
back to a built-in role), or `customRoleId` on `POST /api/members`. See
[ADR 0045](https://github.com/Krithik-m-codes/pinwren/blob/main/docs/adr/0045-custom-roles.md) for the design.
