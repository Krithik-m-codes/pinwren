---
title: Guest portal
description: Where reporters follow the status of what they sent and reply, without an account.
---

Reporters do not need an account to see what happened to their feedback. When a browser has sent at least one item
to a project, the widget's sidebar footer shows **Open my feedback**. It opens the **guest portal** of your Pinwren
server in a new tab: `https://<your-pinwren>/guest/<project key>`.

## What a guest sees

- Their recent items with number, status and kind, in a searchable list. A resolved item says **Fixed in v1.4** when the team names the [release](/docs/dashboard/releases).
- The conversation on each item: the original text and the **public replies** from your team.
- A box to **reply**. The reply is attributed to the original reporter and is always public; it can never become an
  internal note. It does not work on read-only or archived projects.

Guests never see diagnostics, internal comments, teammates' email addresses, the activity timeline or any
management control. If your organisation has [white-label branding](/docs/licensing), the portal uses it; otherwise
it carries the Pinwren branding. Empty, expired, loading and error states are handled, and **Forget this browser's
access** removes the saved access.

## How access works

The portal has no login. A person gets access through a **capability**:

- the widget hands over the **reporter tokens** it kept for that browser (at most 50) in the URL fragment, after the
  `#`, so they are never sent to a server in a request line or a log. The portal reads them, removes the fragment at
  once and remembers access for that project in the browser;
- a reporter email's link carries a signed, expiring page link that opens that one item.

The server requires the project and the matching token or link for every request. Knowing a project key, a
feedback id, someone's email address or having public read switched on never opens other reporters' items. Every
response is private and not cached, and reads and writes have their own rate limits. The routes are
`GET /api/guest/:key/feedback`, `GET /api/guest/:key/feedback/:id` and
`POST /api/guest/:key/feedback/:id/comments`; the tokens travel in the `x-pinwren-reporter-tokens` and
`x-pinwren-page-tokens` headers.

Reporter emails depend on [email](/docs/integrations/email) being set up. See also [visibility](/docs/widget/visibility)
for who gets the widget at all.
