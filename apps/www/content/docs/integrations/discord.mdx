title: Discord
description: Post new feedback, status changes and comments to a Discord channel as rich embeds.
---

Pinwren posts to Discord through a channel **webhook** — no bot to run.

## Set it up

<Steps>
<Step>

In Discord, open the channel's **Edit Channel › Integrations › Webhooks › New Webhook**, name it and **Copy Webhook
URL** (`https://discord.com/api/webhooks/…`).

</Step>
<Step>

In Pinwren, open **Settings › Integrations › Add webhook**, choose the type **Discord**, paste the URL and pick the
events and projects.

</Step>
<Step>

Press **Send test event**. A "Test delivery" message appears in the channel.

</Step>
</Steps>

## What a message looks like

Each event is one message with a short headline — `New feedback: Marketing site #142 Checkout button does nothing`
— and an embed in Pinwren's accent colour:

- **Title**: project, number and title (`Marketing site #142 — Checkout button does nothing`), linking to the item in
  the dashboard.
- **Description**: the feedback, or for `comment.created` the comment.
- **Fields**: Kind, Status and Priority, plus Reporter and Page when known.
- **Timestamp**: when the event happened.

## Safe with untrusted text

- Mentions are disabled for every message (`allowed_mentions: { parse: [] }`), and `@` is broken with a zero-width
  space, so `@everyone` in a report neither pings nor even looks like a mention.
- Markdown characters (`*`, `_`, `~`, `` ` ``, `|`, `>`, `#`, `-`, brackets, `<`, `:`) are backslash-escaped, so text
  can't format the message or fake a link.
- Long text is cut to Discord's limits (about 3,900 characters for the description).

## Good to know

- The webhook URL is a credential: Pinwren shows it masked after you save. Anyone with it can post to the channel —
  delete the webhook in Discord if it leaks.
- Deliveries retry and are logged like [webhooks](/docs/integrations/webhooks#retries), and switch off after three days
  of continuous failure.
- `comment.created` includes **internal notes**; leave it out for channels outside your team.
